    Where the starting numbers come from

    Stem costs: Sam's Club bulk roses ($1.05 to $1.80/stem) and Whole Blossoms (carnations $0.35 to $1.00, garden roses about $5.56).

    Stems per piece: FiftyFlowers (bridal 25 to 36, bridesmaid 15 to 25, boutonniere and corsage 1 to 3).

    Shop prices: Spedale's, Flowers By Rodney, Rickey Heroman's, Rent a Bloom national survey. No Lafayette shop posts flower girl or altar prices, so those shop numbers are estimates.

    Wages: Lafayette floral design jobs about $17.12/hr (ZipRecruiter, Sept 2026); national median $17.96/hr (BLS).

    Deposit and contract terms: Hana Florist POS. Permit: LDAF florists page.

    Minutes per piece are estimates. Time yourself on a practice bouquet and boutonniere and put your real minutes in.
